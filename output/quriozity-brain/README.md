# Brain subject and contrast studies

These standalone render recipes and images preserve the brain studies from the
workspace. The `brain-white` recipe uses the procedural brain; the
`brain-realistic-white` recipes use Objaverse mesh
`0aa0e33c5c854d1bab7bac9e1c7acaec`. The named variants retain the successive
lighting and contrast choices for comparison.

These files are subject studies, not finished reels, and do not change production
defaults or replace the required reel reviews. The owner's private animation
recording and extracted reference evidence are not included.
