# The Sky That Looked Empty

- The December 1995 Hubble Deep Field campaign near the Big Dipper acquired 342 separate WFPC2 exposures in four passbands over ten days. NASA reports about 3,000 galaxies in the field. The four filters record different wavelength ranges. [NASA Hubble Deep Field image](https://science.nasa.gov/image-detail/full-wfpc2-mosaic-full-resolution/) · [NASA campaign report](https://science.nasa.gov/missions/hubble/hubbles-deepest-view-of-the-universe-unveils-bewildering-galaxies-across-billions-of-years/)
- The paper describes the field selection, small offsets between exposures, data registration, image combination and galaxy photometry. The animated exposures are simulated from a crop of the final image with positional offsets and independent noise. Equal averaging is a teaching example. The team used additional calibration, outlier rejection and weighted image reconstruction. [Williams et al., 1996](https://doi.org/10.1086/118105)
- The full-resolution mosaic credits R. Williams, the Hubble Deep Field Team and NASA. The telescope photograph is from the STS-125 crew in May 2009 and appears with its date in the film. [Mosaic description and credits](https://science.nasa.gov/asset/hubble/hubble-deep-field-image-at-full-resolution/) · [Telescope photograph](https://science.nasa.gov/image-detail/hubble-space-telescope-hst-7/)
