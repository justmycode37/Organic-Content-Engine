# The Bump Beside the Peak

The opening and the subsequent close-ups plot the ERS team's Eureka! reduction of the July 2022 NIRSpec PRISM transmission spectrum. Positions and uncertainty bars come from the published table. Transit depth is expressed as a percentage. The light curves group six consecutive measurements after division by the published systematics model. Time is shown in hours from mid-transit. The planet, atmospheric cross sections and molecules are illustrations. The 2% and 2.1% comparison is a worked example.

- The measured carbon dioxide band and the underlying figure data. [JWST Transiting Exoplanet Community Early Release Science Team](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05269-w) · [Numerical data and model files](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6959427).
- The model comparison uses the original release’s ScCHIMERA model. Residuals subtract linearly interpolated model depth from the observed depth. The photon cups, detector drift, spectral-resolution strips and chemistry diagrams are illustrations. [Data and model archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6959427).
- Independent instrument modes and the narrow absorption feature. The side-by-side view uses the published PRISM Eureka! and G395H weighted-average measurements with their own 1σ errors on matched depth scales. G395H depths are converted from fractional depth to percent. [G395H data](https://zenodo.org/records/7185300). [Alderson et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05591-3).
- The sulfur dioxide identification and the mechanism inferred from photochemical models. [Tsai et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-05902-2).
- The MIRI reveal uses the Figure 2 Eureka! spectrum, with transit depths and 1σ errors converted from ppm to percent. For comparison it shows the best-fit PICASO curve from Extended Data Figure 3 and the published curve for that same model with SO₂ opacity removed. [Figure 2 data](https://zenodo.org/records/10055845). [Powell et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07040-9).
- The morning and evening limbs in a subsequent transit analysis. [NASA Webb science team](https://science.nasa.gov/missions/webb/nasas-webb-investigates-eternal-sunrises-sunsets-on-distant-world/).
