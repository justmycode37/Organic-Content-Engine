# v1 review digest (4 Luna reviewers). Scores: visuals 4/10, story 5–6/10, sound 5.5/10.

## Top problems (all reviewers)
1. Payoff doesn't happen: no loop, no press closing, no compression, no replay into the opening; the last 2 s are a near-static end card.
2. Several beats hold still / pose instead of act (wait, collateral call, forced buyback, minimarket ~2.9/255 change).
3. Actions implied not shown: sale, buyback, new buyer, other sellers covering must be unmistakable gestures with cause → effect.
4. Frame feels small: big dark fields in shots 5, 10, 12; bottom 630 px black. Need more foreground passes / parallax.
5. Framing: Hedgy clipped at 7.25 s (vaultlock, left), 12.9 s (crowdrush, right), 18.9 s (forcedbuyback, left); "CHEAPER" clipped at left 5.3–6.0 s.
6. Transitions use a preset instead of a motivated prop match; one-frame full-panel WHITE flash in `match` (4.33 s, 25.5 s) reads as a strobe; `flip` midpoint 88% black.
7. Captions too fragmented (one word visible at a time, others too dim). Show phrase chunks up to ~6 words; active word highlighted, the rest still readable.
8. Sound: 137 SFX in 29 s (4.7/s), up to 10 overlapping — clutter. Music bed ~30 dB under voice (inaudible). No vacuum before "squeeze"; weak end button.
9. Ticket paper is warm ivory — must be neutral grey/white.

## Transition notes
1→2 slice chops across Hedgy: carry the ticket's perforated edge across lens instead. 2→3 match: kill the white frame. 3→4 zoomthrough shows the vault as an inset: aim at the lower shelf whose edge becomes the vault wheel. 4→5 iris off-centre: align wheel and clock centres (transitionAt). 5→6 push: let the clock hand sweep into a spotlight. 6→7 whip: a fan's palm leads across lens. 7→8 push: last ticket leaves its window and draws the first red segment. 8→9 whip up: keep the line endpoint onscreen and land it on the obligation pan. 9→10 shutter: the drawer itself closes across frame. 10→11 zoomthrough: centre on the kiosk slot. 11→12 flip: midpoint near-black — raise min scale or replace. 12→13 push up: line climbs out of rack into first booth. 13→14 match: no white flash; carry red path around the tower into the press.

## Bold ideas
- Ticket as camera portal: push through its perforation; holes recur as windows/shutters/booth openings; punch back through one at the end.
- The tether is the film's spine: one continuous obligation line that becomes receipt edge → red price path → loop.
- Arena reacts like a domino machine.
- Hedgy physical arc: curious/confident → pulled off balance by the tightening tether → sprint → startled landing and point.
- End with a real squeeze and seamless replay into frame 1.

## Sound fixes (mixer reviewer)
Aim 65–75 SFX total, ≤3 active at once (one texture, one action, one accent); ≤1 short transient within 120 ms of the main hit. Remove transition zap/impact stacking at 4.31 and 25.48. Per shot: borrowedasset 10 cues → 3 (entrance whip, sells impact, snap on "his"); pricestair: one thud on "bets", two ticks, ONE tap on "cheaper"; vaultlock: one paper on "money", one vault latch on "collateral"; announcement: one crisp hit + 3 soft pulses; crowdrush: two footfalls + snap on "last"; priceride: one low heartbeat on "losses"; collateralcall: low warning knock (not bright ding), clack on "collateral", quiet two-pulse alarm; buybackhandoff: one 350–450 ms stereo sweep; minimarket: one lift tick; demandfeedback: three covering clacks rising +1.5 dB each, short build; squeezerocket: one arrival whoosh, three servos, sub-drop 300 ms before "squeeze", music dip −12 dB for 250 ms before, impact on "squeeze", warm two-note button ~0.9 s before end, +6 dB, 400–500 ms tail.
Music: normalise the bed to −20 LUFS before mixing, bed gain 0.35–0.45, voice duck 3:1, threshold ~0.05, attack 12 ms, release 200 ms; post-duck music 8–12 dB under narration. Palette leans too much on narrow synth tones (generic UI pack): favour richer foley (paper, metal, crowd) for story actions.
