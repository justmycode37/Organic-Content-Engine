# Hedgy implementation status

The hosted application presents independent accounts and the Hedgy brand policy. Its durable queue schedules production ahead of each account's local posting time, leases work to the account worker, stores finished MP4s in R2, and publishes through account-scoped Instagram credentials. Rendering and paid inference run in the installed Mac account worker, outside a user browser tab. The Mac must be awake and online.

OpenAI and ElevenLabs keys are server/worker secrets. The owner-only account UI offers Instagram connection and truthful readiness. A Meta developer app's Instagram Client ID and secret are still required, followed by Instagram professional-account authorization. These cannot be inferred from an Instagram username or login. Missing credentials block scheduled paid generation and publishing.

Unpublished account migrations were consolidated into one generated schema-only migration before first deployment. Applied migrations 0000–0005 remain unchanged; Hedgy's initial account and current brand policy are seeded through application DML rather than data migrations.

Jobs have exclusive leases and heartbeats. Interrupted paid generation requires inspection rather than blindly paying again. Cached specs and audio are reused. Ambiguous publication is marked for attention rather than repeated automatically.

## Owner feedback, revision 2

The canonical account rules now live in `worker/hedgy-policy.ts` and are applied to each API production. Migration 0007 records the account change. The account API presents the current policy even before a stored brand refresh. Hedgy videos use monochrome sparse semantic scenes, no persistent wordmark or logo, no accent, no disclaimer or greeting. Hedgy is an optional actor in at most two scenes. The motion schema rejects incompatible Hedgy specs; the compositor suppresses chrome and background decoration and forces a monochrome theme. Text and captions have distinct vertical bands; contrasting opaque shielding handles intentional overlaps.

Revision 3 supersedes the strict monochrome interpretation: keep complex shaded 3D pixel animations and camera motion with fewer simultaneous panels and labels. Hedgy retains natural brown colouring. Red and green are allowed only for stock direction, gains and losses. The base remains black and white. Migration 0008 persists this correction; the renderer uses explicit semantic palettes rather than decorative accents. Stock and option calculations are hypothetical per-share or 100-share examples as stated in the script. Voice captions and numeric labels occupy separate bands.

Revision 4: Hedgy himself is always a naturally coloured **2D pixel sprite**, including in otherwise 3D scenes. Scene camera clipping is applied before transforms at y=1260, transitions are clipped to the same animation region, captions are clipped to y=1320–1650 and shifted down within it. A 60px empty band separates the regions, including during camera movement. This is a renderer guarantee rather than a prompt-only instruction. Migration 0009 records it.

Revision 5: fit and wrap every caption completely inside its exclusive layout region; captions are no longer clipped. The layout accounts for animated word scaling and stroke padding. Hedgy remains a 2D character but now changes action, pose and expression: recoil at a loss, walk and push a shield, blink and move his feet and paws. Surrounding objects retain shaded 3D pixel animation. Migration 0010 stores these final preferences.

Revision 6: a faster, mechanism-led animation vocabulary replaces unrelated turning icons. Scenes have an explicit action (share purchase, crash, put contract, expiry, payout, net loss, upside or premium). `sync` markers name narration words; the audio arranger resolves their actual TTS start times into scene-local `events`. The renderer uses those timestamps for visual action and synchronized sound cues. Shots target 2–4 seconds with meaningful changes every 0.4–0.8 seconds. Hedgy narration defaults to 1.2 speed. Migration 0011 records the account preference.

Revision 7: narration must form one relatable, causally connected paragraph rather than a series of separate punchlines. Avoid arithmetic and unfamiliar terminology; explain one idea with an analogy and carry it through the film. Subjects use more of the safe animation region, with camera pushes and zoom-through transitions. The new story action vocabulary connects phone protection, concern about a stock, a protective contract, a softened loss, the cost when markets rise, and a clear final takeaway. Migration 0012 records the preference. `MOTION_SPEECH_TEMPO` can ease an existing take without new provider charges, while scaling every word timestamp to preserve synchronization.

Revision 8: preserve the relatable opening analogy. Reusable phone geometry now includes a stepped chassis, inset glass, lock-screen clock, status icons, speaker/front camera, notification, dock controls, side buttons, charging port/speaker holes, antenna seams and rear camera lenses. Hedgy's opening renders it at a finer pixel resolution inside a separate sliding case. Transitions and their sound cues are authored independently; `transitionSound: 'none'` produces a silent cut. The story uses zoom/rise, wipe/tick, hard cut/silence, shutter/glitch, and assembly/shimmer. Scene cues are tied to tactile/financial actions instead of replaying the same swish and tick sequence. Camera exit zoom is enabled only before a zoom transition. Migration 0013 persists the account preference.


## Reproduce the final analogy reel

The checked-in recipe is `chatgpt-site/motion-examples/hedgy-protection.json`. Generated MP4s, HTML previews, provider traces and cost logs remain local under ignored `artifacts/`. With an existing local `.env.motion` audio credential, run:

```sh
cd chatgpt-site
MOTION_LEAD=.04 MOTION_TAIL=.12 MOTION_SPEECH_TEMPO=.85 node --env-file=.env.motion scripts/motion-audio.mjs motion-examples/hedgy-protection.json /tmp/hedgy-protection.mp4
```

Rendering may incur audio-provider charges when the corresponding cache entries do not exist. No API keys or generated media are checked in. Committing these changes does not deploy the Site or connect the remaining unattended generation/publishing integration.

## Motion v3 (shot library) — showcase "The ticket that took off"

v3 replaces "object on black with a headline" with full-bleed, choreographed 3D pixel-art shots. Architecture and ownership are in `docs/motion-v3.md`; the look rules in `docs/shot-bible.md`; review digests per iteration in `docs/reviews/`.

- **Engine** (`src/motion/three.ts`): lit 7-step material ramps (mono/bone/steel/ink/chrome/glass/paper; brown/red/green semantic only), key+rim+ambient light, specular glints, outlines/creases, ground shadows, fog, full perspective camera with near-plane clipping (fly-throughs), explode/slice builds, instancing.
- **Assets** (`src/motion/assets/`): 63 parametric finance models with named animatable parts.
- **Hedgy actor** (`src/motion/actor/hedgy.ts`): redesigned 2D sprite, 17 poses with timing landmarks and cue helpers.
- **Player** (`src/motion/player.ts`, `fx/transitions.ts`, `fx/rig.ts`): two-scene compositing transitions (push, whip, zoomthrough, shatter, pixelsort, melt, iris, cube, flip, dissolve, slice, ink, match), each with its own sound design; camera rig; full-bleed 1080×1290 animation region for `shot` scenes; floor reflection and light grade under the caption band.
- **FX / env / viz** (`fx/particles.ts`, `fx/post.ts`, `env/`, `viz/`): deterministic particles, environments, post effects, finance charts, kinetic type, word-timed phrase captions.
- **Shots** (`src/motion/shots/`): scene type `shot` with narration-synced `events`; `shots/catalog.ts` is the worker-safe catalog the generator uses.
- **Audio** (`scripts/motion-audio.mjs`, `scripts/audio/`): designed sound for all 38 cue kinds (synth + ElevenLabs foley), cue governor (priority, ≤3 overlapping), sidechained music with event-driven lifts, squeeze vacuum/impact, end button, reel-level caption chunking; −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP.
- **Review kit**: `node scripts/review-reel.mjs <reel.mp4> <outDir>` writes frame sheets, per-second motion (dead-hold detection) and loudness/spectrogram reports for reviewers.

Render the showcase:

```sh
cd chatgpt-site
MOTION_VOICE_SPEED=1.15 MOTION_LEAD=.04 MOTION_TAIL=.14 node --env-file=.env.motion scripts/motion-audio.mjs motion-examples/hedgy-squeeze.json /tmp/hedgy-squeeze.mp4
```

Note: this film features Hedgy in most shots because he is the actor of the example (policy: "use Hedgy only when he is an actor"). The schema's two-scene mascot cap applies to the legacy `hedgy` scene type only.
