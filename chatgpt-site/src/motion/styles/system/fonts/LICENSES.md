# Bundled typefaces

- `Geist.ttf`: Geist variable sans, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/geist . Download: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/geist/Geist%5Bwght%5D.ttf . Full licence in `OFL-Geist.txt`.
- `GeistMono.ttf`: Geist Mono variable monospace, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/geistmono . Download: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/geistmono/GeistMono%5Bwght%5D.ttf . Full licence in `OFL-GeistMono.txt`.

Both fonts permit embedding and redistribution under their included OFL licences. Neither font is modified. Runtime family aliases are `System Geist` and `System Geist Mono`; fallback stacks use Inter/Helvetica/Arial and SF Mono/Consolas/monospace. Downloads: 11 November 2026.
