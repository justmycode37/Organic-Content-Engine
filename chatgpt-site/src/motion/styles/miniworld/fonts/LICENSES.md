# MINI WORLD font licences

Both font files are downloaded from the Google Fonts repository, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, and may be embedded. The unmodified full licence texts are included alongside the fonts.

| Local file | Family | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nunito.ttf | Nunito variable, weight 200–1000 | https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/nunito/Nunito%5Bwght%5D.ttf | SIL OFL 1.1; Nunito-OFL.txt |
| DM-Sans.ttf | DM Sans variable, optical size and weight 100–1000 | https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/dmsans/DMSans%5Bopsz,wght%5D.ttf | SIL OFL 1.1; DM-Sans-OFL.txt |

Nunito is used for world signage. DM Sans is registered locally as `Miniworld Sans` and used for captions and information boards. Each has an explicit Arial/sans-serif fallback. No remote font request is made while rendering.
