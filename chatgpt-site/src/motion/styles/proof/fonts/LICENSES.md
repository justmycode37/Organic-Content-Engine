# Embedded fonts

- **Space Grotesk**, variable weight 300–700, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/spacegrotesk
  Binary: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/spacegrotesk/SpaceGrotesk%5Bwght%5D.ttf
  Full license and copyright notices: `SpaceGrotesk-OFL.txt`.
- **IBM Plex Mono**, Regular 400, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ibmplexmono
  Binary: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/ibmplexmono/IBMPlexMono-Regular.ttf
  Full license and copyright notices: `IBMPlexMono-OFL.txt`.

Files were downloaded without modification. Internal CSS aliases are `Proof
Grotesk` and `Proof Mono`; font fallbacks are Helvetica Neue/Arial/sans-serif and
SF Mono/Consolas/monospace. Neither alias renames the distributed font binaries.
No hand-marker font is needed: marker strokes are procedural canvas paths.
