# Silicon Footnote YouTube thumbnail research

24 November 2026 · AI and technology news, reporting, and adjacent explainers · 10:00–15:00

Inspect the cover as part of the package. The title promises an answer, the cover gives the viewer one thing to recognize, and the opening backs both up with evidence.

## What the reference covers do

The observations refer to the covers available at the research snapshot. A current thumbnail can differ from the one a viewer previously saw. ## Applied to the producer

- **Subject cover.** One independently sourced still with its reuse credit, or a large original illustration chosen for the story. A dark text field keeps the key phrase readable over varied imagery.
- **Evidence cover.** A separate light layout foregrounds the mechanism or consequence. An original source card shows the opening exhibit's source title. This gives the viewer a different reason to click while keeping the episode title fixed.
- **Copy.** Fresh scripts draft the title and both lines together. Each cover gets one to five words with distinct wording. The prompt asks for a claim the opening can support and keeps qualifiers with quantitative or company-specific claims. In editorial review, compare both covers with the title and first 30 seconds. A 320 × 180 preview should make the subject and main phrase clear without zooming.
- **Assets.** The renderer reads the script, source pack, and separately downloaded stills. Both designs are composed specifically for YouTube at 3840 × 2160 and exported as individual JPEGs under 2 MiB. The video file and its frame cache are outside the renderer's inputs. Competitor artwork is research reference material. [YouTube thumbnail specifications](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431).
- **Selection.** Preview or download both designs under **Episodes → YouTube thumbnails**. Keep the title fixed for a thumbnail-only test. Use Studio watch-time share for the test and inspect 30-second retention to see whether the opening delivered the package's promise. [YouTube A/B testing](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400) · [YouTube retention](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415).
